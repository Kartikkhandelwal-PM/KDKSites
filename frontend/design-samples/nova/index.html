About the Practice

Eighteen years. One standard: accuracy.

Priya Mehta & Co. was established in 2007 in Bandra, Mumbai. From a two-person practice to a nine-member firm, the growth has been driven by one thing: clients who trust us enough to refer us to everyone they know.

We serve salaried individuals, freelancers, MSMEs, listed companies, and NRI clients across seven countries. The same attention to detail applies regardless of the size of the filing.

Every client type, every situation
Salaried professionals, freelancers, NRIs, partnership firms, Pvt Ltd companies. Eighteen years means we have seen every edge case, and we file without guesswork.
Current on every rule change
Finance Act updates, GST council circulars, new ITR schema releases. We track all of it so you do not have to, and you always benefit from the latest positions.
You talk to the person doing the work
